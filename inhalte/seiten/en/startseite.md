## Welcome to Mentis Uno

Sometimes you have the feeling, over a long period of time, that something doesn't quite fit – at school, at university, at work or in your relationships with others. A careful psychological assessment can help you understand your own experiences and behaviour better. It creates a reliable basis for decisions: about support, treatment or the way forward.

We take time for your concerns. We work with scientifically grounded methods and explain our results in a way that is useful to you.

We are a team of two. We discuss our assessments with each other – so two professional perspectives go into your report, with you at the centre.
