## What is it about?

Psychological difficulties are varied and often overlap. A clinical psychological assessment evaluates them systematically. It clarifies whether a mental health condition is present, which one it is and how pronounced it is. The result is a well-founded basis for decisions, for example about psychotherapy or specialist medical treatment.

We assess a broad range of questions, for example:

- Low mood, exhaustion and mood swings
- Anxiety, worries and compulsions
- Consequences of distressing experiences
- Difficulties in dealing with emotions and in relationships
- Problems with eating behaviour
- Emotional and behavioural difficulties in children and adolescents

## Who is the assessment for?

- Adults, children and adolescents with emotional difficulties who would like to understand them better
- People who would like to gain clarity before starting psychotherapy
- People who would like a second opinion on an existing diagnosis

## How does the assessment work?

1. **Initial consultation:** We get to know you and your concerns.
2. **Structured interviews:** We record your difficulties systematically according to established diagnostic criteria.
3. **Questionnaires and tests:** Standardised procedures complete the picture – for children also assessments by parents and school.
4. **Report and recommendations:** You receive a written report and discuss the results and possible next steps with us.

## Good to know

We offer assessment, not crisis support or ongoing treatment. If you are feeling very unwell right now, please use the support services on this page.
