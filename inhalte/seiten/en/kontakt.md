## What happens next

After your enquiry we will get back to you by email or phone. In a short conversation we clarify your concerns, the process and the expected costs. Then we arrange the appointments.

Our services are for self-paying clients. Whether your private health insurance covers the costs depends on your plan – please check this with your insurer in advance.
